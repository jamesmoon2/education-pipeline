# Course Specification: Thinking in Feedback Loops

A 15-minute introductory course for a project manager who learns best from
concrete, everyday examples. Gardening and project-delivery scenarios carry
the concepts; no systems-modeling background is assumed.

## Learning Outcomes

- Identify reinforcing and balancing feedback in a familiar system.
- Map a feedback loop by tracing a changing quantity back to itself.
- Choose an intervention that accounts for feedback structure and delays.

## Approach

The course follows the conceptual-foundations blueprint: each concept is
introduced through a familiar situation, checked with a short interaction,
and connected back to the learner's own projects. Quantitative
stock-and-flow modeling is deliberately deferred to a follow-on course.
